Frequently asked questions

Where are my results stored?

On your device. Every report you save, the values in it, your concerns, and your optional profile are written to your device with iOS Data Protection and excluded from iCloud backups. There is no account, and we have no servers that hold this data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does my report get sent anywhere?

Its pages do, once, to be read. When you scan or import a report, Labora sends the page images to our extraction service and on to Google’s Gemini service to turn them into text. Nothing is kept there, and nothing is used to train a model. The values come back to your device for you to confirm, and only then are they saved. If you would rather nothing left your device at all, you can enter a report’s values by hand instead. The Privacy Policy sets this out in full.

Why do I have to confirm every value?

Because an automatic read of a medical document should never be taken on trust. Labora shows you what it read so you can check it against your original report before anything is saved. If a value looks wrong, correct it.

Which labs does it work with?

Any of them. Labora reads a printed report or a PDF rather than connecting to a portal, so results from different labs, different providers, and different years all end up in the same place. It always shows the reference range your own lab printed, not a range of our own.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Labora is completely free - there is no paywall, no in-app purchase, and no paid tier. Every feature is available to everyone.

Is Labora medical advice?

No. Labora is a general-wellness tool for understanding and tracking your own results. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it is not a substitute for professional care. Always discuss your results with a qualified healthcare professional. See our Health Disclaimer.

What is the “beyond standard ranges” research layer?

Standard reference ranges describe where most people fall, which is not always the same as where future risk is lowest. Some markers have a live research argument about that distinction. Labora shows those arguments in a clearly labeled layer - what the standard says, what the frontier argues, the counter-signals, and the sources - graded by how strong the evidence is. It is kept deliberately separate from the interpretation of your own results, and it is reading material, not advice.

How do I delete my data?

Delete any report from inside the app, or remove the app to remove everything it stored. Because we hold no copy, that is the whole of it.
