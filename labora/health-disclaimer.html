Health Disclaimer

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Labora is a general-wellness tool for understanding and tracking your own lab results. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional care. Using Labora does not create a doctor-patient or other professional relationship.

Labora does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. It does not screen for, or monitor, any disease. It does not tell you whether a result is good or bad, and it does not set a target for you.

What Labora shows you comes from two places: the values and reference ranges printed on the report you provide, and general reference information about what each marker is. The ranges are your own lab’s, not ours. Different labs use different ranges for the same test, and a result should always be read against the range printed alongside it.

Labora reads your report automatically and can misread it. That is why you confirm every value before it is saved. Check what the app read against your original report, and treat the original report, not this app, as the record.

The “beyond standard ranges” research layer is reading material about active scientific debate, not guidance. It is clearly labeled, graded by how strong the evidence is, presented with its counter-signals, and kept separate from any interpretation of your own results. A marker having an interesting research debate attached to it says nothing about your reading.

The questions Labora drafts for your clinician are general prompts to help you talk through your results. They are not a diagnosis, not a list of things that are wrong, and not a substitute for what your clinician decides to ask.

Do not start, stop, or change any medication, treatment, or course of care based on this app. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your individual situation. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Reference information and the research behind it change over time. Labora makes no warranty that its data is current, complete, or accurate. Labora is currently offered only in the United States, and its reference data reflects U.S. sources.
