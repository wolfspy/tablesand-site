How it works

From a wall of acronyms to one clear picture.

1

Scan or import

Photograph a printed result or import a PDF, from any lab, any year. Labora reads the values so you do not have to retype them.

2

Confirm every value

You check what was read before anything is saved. Nothing is treated as final until you say so.

3

Understand and track

Every marker explained against your own lab's range, related to the markers around it, and charted over time.

Every marker

All your results, in one place.

Your blood history is scattered across portals that will never talk to each other. Labora merges every report you import into one list, grouped the way a lab panels them, with the date each reading was taken.

Understand

Every number, in plain language.

Not a wall of acronyms. Each marker is explained in words you can actually use, placed against the reference range your own lab printed:

  • ✓What the marker is and why it gets ordered
  • ✓Where your result sits in your lab's own range
  • ✓Common things that can push a reading up or down
  • ✓The sources behind every explanation
Connections

Your markers don't live alone.

Inflammation can inflate ferritin and hide low iron. ApoB and LDL particle number can disagree. Labora shows the relationships a report leaves out, each one cited, so a single number in isolation stops misleading you.

Over time

One result is a snapshot.

The useful thing about repeated tests is the direction. Labora charts every reading you have for a marker on a single timeline, normalizing units across labs so a value in mmol/L still lines up with one in mg/dL.

Concerns

Group the markers you follow.

Build a concern for heart, thyroid, iron, or blood sugar, and the whole app narrows to just those markers. Start from a template or pick your own, and see at a glance which ones no report has covered yet.

Your appointment

Walk in with better questions.

Labora drafts the questions worth raising about a report and lets you share them. General prompts to help you talk through your results, not a script and not a diagnosis.

Beyond standard ranges

Where the research questions "normal."

Reference ranges describe where most people fall, not necessarily where future risk is lowest. A clearly labeled research layer shows where that distinction is argued: what the standard says, what the frontier argues, the counter-signals, and the sources behind both, each graded by how strong its evidence is. Kept separate from your lab's own interpretation, and never presented as advice.

🔒

Private by design.

Your results are stored on your device, encrypted at rest and kept out of iCloud backups. There is no account to create. Reading a report sends its pages to our extraction service once, to turn the image into text - nothing is kept there, and nothing is used to train a model.
